# Inter-basin transfers and neighbouring dams for the Souss-Massa basin

Research note. Status as of 2026-11-19. Sources were read on that date. Each fact carries a verification mark:

- **FETCH**: page was opened and read with WebFetch on 2026-11-19.
- **DOWNLOAD**: PDF was downloaded to `/private/tmp/srmsm-transfers-research/` and read with `pdftotext`.
- **SNIPPET**: only a search-result excerpt was seen. The page was not opened, or the fetch failed (HTTP 403). Treat as unverified.
- **CONFLICT**: sources disagree. Both values are listed.

Labels used below: **OPERATIONAL**, **UNDER WORKS**, **PLANNED (date)**, **STUDY**, and **NOT FOUND**. Nothing here is a route or a figure that a source did not state.

---

## 1. Summary

- The north-south imbalance is large. On 18 Nov 2026 the Sebou basin held 3,959 Mm³, about 35% of national storage (11,276 Mm³). Souss-Massa (383 Mm³) and Draa-Oued Noun (416 Mm³) together held about 7%.
- The only operational inter-basin transfer found is Sebou to Bouregreg (phase 1, Aug 2023). Its phase 2 (Bouregreg to Oum Er-Rbia, with a later extension toward Al Massira) is planned, with no confirmed completion date.
- No existing or planned physical link between Souss-Massa and Tensift, Draa-Oued Noun or Guelmim was found in any source. Souss-Massa's interconnection projects are intra-basin.
- The only explicit drinking-water priority wording in the consolidated Law 36-15 is Art. 126, which applies when a scarcity state is declared. Art. 91 lists domestic, industrial and agricultural needs without ranking them. No dead-storage definition was found.

## 3. (b) Existing, under-construction and planned transfers

### 3.1 Sebou to Bouregreg ("autoroute de l'eau" phase 1). OPERATIONAL since Aug 2023

- Flow: 15 m³/s. Verification: FETCH (Hespress 26 Mar 2025, https://fr.hespress.com/417090-417090.html; Le360 17 Apr 2026, https://fr.le360.ma/economie/mix-hydrique-comment-le-maroc-pousse-plus-loin-linterconnexion-des-bassins_5WN7TUNYB5BXZPWF4HRBZPOJHY/).
- Pipeline: 67 km of steel pipes and two pumping stations (Hespress, FETCH). Le360 17 Apr 2026 says the phase 1 link is "built late 2022 to Aug 2023", launched Dec 2022, with progressive service from 28 Aug 2023 (Le360 29 Aug 2026, FETCH). Some search snippets give 66.5 km (SNIPPET); the 67 km figure is the one in the FETCH-verified source.
- Cumulative volume transferred Aug 2023 to Dec 2025: about 953 Mm³, cost about 6 billion MAD (Le360 17 Apr 2026, FETCH). Note this is cumulative, not annual.
- CONFLICT: annual volume estimates range from 350-450 Mm³ (Le Matin, SNIPPET) to about 500 Mm³ (Hespress, SNIPPET). The cumulative figure above is the verified one.
- Named conduits or canals: none in the verified sources.

### 3.2 Sebou to Bouregreg phase 2 (Bouregreg to Oum Er-Rbia). PLANNED

- Target: 800 Mm³/yr for the extension, raising total to about 1,200 Mm³/yr (Le360 29 Aug 2026, FETCH).
- Flow: Sebou-Bouregreg raised to 45 m³/s; Bouregreg-Oum Er-Rbia at 30 m³/s (Hespress 26 Mar 2025, FETCH).
- Length: "près de 300 kilomètres" for the whole project (Le360 29 Aug 2026, FETCH). Le360 17 Apr 2026 (FETCH) gives "about 210 km" for phase 2. The 209.6 km figure is in SNIPPET (Le Matin, Le Desk). **CONFLICT** on length (210 km vs 300 km); the source does not say whether they describe the same section.
- Phase 2 PPP partners named in a Le Desk snippet: TAQA Morocco, Nareva and FM6I (SNIPPET, not verified).
- Scope (Le360 29 Aug 2026, FETCH): phase 1 of the new work targets Oum Er-Rbia (Settat, Berrechid, areas south of Casablanca) and irrigation of about 176,000 ha. A "phase ultérieure" would extend toward the Al Massira dam.
- Status and date:
  - Hespress 26 Mar 2025 (FETCH): "bientôt lancée", with studies due March 2025.
  - Le360 17 Apr 2026 (FETCH): "Son lancement est programmé pour la fin de l'année 2026."
  - Le360 29 Aug 2026 (FETCH): "dans le pipe" (in preparation). No start or completion date.
  - Le Matin (SNIPPET, title: "Les études pour relier Sebou à Al Massira finalisées en 2026"). Fetch returned 403.
  - Ministry minister's Oct 2025 statement "concrétisée d'ici fin décembre 2025" (Medias24, SNIPPET; fetch returned 403). Whether that deadline was met is not verified. Le360 (17 Apr 2026) gives the phase 2 launch as end-2026, so the phase had not started by that date.
- Bouregreg to Al Massira link, 30 m³/s, 209.6 km (SNIPPET only). Treat as unverified.

### 3.3 Phase 3 (Laou to Loukkos to Sebou). PLANNED, no date

- Volume: "près de 1,2 milliard de m³" per year. "Actuellement planifiée", no date (Le360 17 Apr 2026, FETCH).

### 3.4 Oum Er-Rbia to Marrakech (Tensift). Mix of existing canal and PLANNED extension

- **Existing, Rocade canal (Oum Er-Rbia to Tensift):** SNIPPET only: about 300 Mm³/yr over 120 km, built in the 1970s. Not fetched. Use with caution.
- **ONEE supply from the Rocade (budget item, PLF 2027):** 285 MDH to supply El Kelaa des Sraghna and El Attaouia "from the Rocade canal" (Le360 26 Oct 2026, FETCH). This is a planned budget line, not a new link.
- **Al Massira extension toward Marrakech. PLANNED, no date.** The later phase of the Sebou-Oum Er-Rbia network "en direction du barrage Al Massira" (Le360 29 Aug 2026, FETCH). Marrakech and Tensift are not named in that article. SNIPPET-only sources say the Al Massira link would supply Marrakech (FM6E) and that "70 Mm³ per year" from Al Massira would cover the city until 2030. These are not verified.
- **Marrakech and Haouz drinking water:** the 2021-2022 emergency plan aimed to meet all Marrakech drinking-water needs from Massira (SNIPPET, unverified).
- **Safi desalinated water to Marrakech:** a Le360 headline says the project "entre dans sa phase finale" (SNIPPET). Not verified and not a dam-to-dam transfer. Not checked further.

## 5. (d) Legal and operational constraints

Source: Loi n° 36-15 relative à l'eau, consolidated version of 30/7/2025 (SGG), DOWNLOAD (`/private/tmp/srmsm-transfers-research/loi36-15.pdf`). The SGG notes that only the Bulletin Officiel text is authoritative. Amended by Law 05-23 (Feb 2023, SNIPPET).

- **Drinking water priority in scarcity (Art. 126).** When the administration declares a water shortage on a basin agency proposal, it "édicte ... les mesures locales et temporaires en donnant la priorité à l'approvisionnement des populations en quantité nécessaires d'eau potable et en tenant compte des besoins en eau du cheptel". This is the only explicit drinking-water priority wording found in the consolidated text. It applies to declared scarcity. No general priority over irrigation was found. DOWNLOAD (verified).
- **Basin master plan contents (Art. 91, DOWNLOAD).** The plan directeur d'aménagement intégré covers "l'affectation des eaux mobilisables aux différents usages potentiels" and aims at "la satisfaction d'une manière durable et à moindre coût, des besoins en eau domestique, industrielle, agricole et des autres secteurs". The text gives no ranking between these uses. Art. 25 requires authorisations to be compatible with the plan "lorsqu'il existe". Art. 92 sets the plan at 30 years or more, revised every 10 years.
- **Requisition power (Art. 128).** Without amicable agreement, the administration can requisition water resources for drinking-water supply. DOWNLOAD (verified).
- **Basin agency missions (Art. 80).** Within its area, the agency drafts the basin master plan and "le plan de gestion de la pénurie d'eau en cas de sécheresse", manages resources in an integrated way, controls their use, and grants authorisations and concessions. DOWNLOAD (verified).
- **Water police (Art. 131).** Water-police agents are commissioned by the administration, by the basin agencies, and by other public bodies. They are sworn and can record offences. Access to wells and works is set out in Art. 132. DOWNLOAD (verified). Some sources (pseau.org fiche, SNIPPET) say the water police is attached to the basin agencies. The law text does not use that wording: it lists the agencies as one of several commissioning bodies.
- **National water plan (Art. 90).** The plan is at least 30 years, approved by decree, and revised every 10 years. DOWNLOAD (verified).
- **Inter-basin transfers.** No article on inter-basin transfers was found in the consolidated text. The word "transfert" appears only in the context of authorisation or concession transfers (Arts. 32, 35, 43-44) and of water-conveyance pipes (Art. 33). DOWNLOAD (verified by grep).
- **Dead storage (volume mort).** No definition or rule was found in Law 36-15 or in any fetched source. One SNIPPET (Medias24, 4 Apr 2022) reports minister Nizar Baraka saying about 30% of storage in the 148 dams is unusable because of silt. The 30% of 5.3 billion m³ is about 1.6 billion m³, so the two figures are consistent. Whether silt-blocked storage is the same as dead storage is not established.
- **Drinking water share.** Ministry figure (SNIPPET, Le Matin): share of dam water for drinking water rose from 21% (2017-2018) to 54% (2023-2024). Not fetched.

## 6. National programme and plans

- **Programme National d'Approvisionnement en Eau Potable et d'Irrigation 2020-2027.** Verification: DOWNLOAD of the ABHSM press release (16 Jan 2026), which states the programme aims to "satisfaire au moins 80 % des besoins en eau d'irrigation, à accélérer la réalisation des barrages, des projets de transfert interbassins ainsi que le développement des stations de dessalement de l'eau de mer, afin d'atteindre une capacité de mobilisation supérieure à 1,7 milliard de m3 à l'horizon 2030." Cost of over 115.4 billion MAD (115,400 MDH) is SNIPPET (Medias24). Signed 13 January 2020 (SNIPPET). Not fetched from the programme document itself.
- **Projects scheduled for commissioning 2029-2030** in Oriental, Souss-Massa, Tangier, Guelmim-Tan-Tan and Rabat (SNIPPET, Le Matin, PLF 2027). Not fetched.
- **Plan National de l'Eau 2020-2050.** Decree 2.18.339 (June 2018, SNIPPET). Mobilise 4.5 billion m³ additional by 2050 (SNIPPET). Official text not found. Law 36-15 Art. 90 gives the statutory framework (verified).
- **Deficit by basin.** Souss-Massa, Moulouya and Sakia El Hamra-Oued Eddahab are said to have a deficit of 50-65% (SNIPPET, undated). Not fetched.
- **Emergency plan 2022.** Adds Moulouya, Oum Er-Rbia and Tensift as deficit basins (SNIPPET, Medias24 7 Mar 2022).
