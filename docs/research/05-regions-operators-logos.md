# 05 - Regional operators (water, electricity, sanitation) and logos, 12 regions

Status date: 2026-11-19. Method: web search (standard mode), WebFetch and curl of the sources cited. No official SRM domain resolved. Statuses come from secondary press and recruitment notices unless marked otherwise. Anything marked UNVERIFIED is not confirmed.

## Key findings

- The regional operators are Sociétés Régionales Multiservices (SRM), created under law 83-21 (dahir 1.23.53, July 2023), one per region. They replace the earlier régies and delegated operators.
- Phased go-live as reported: Casablanca-Settat 1 Oct 2024; Souss-Massa 15 Oct 2024; Marrakech-Safi launch 2 Nov 2024; Oriental created 15 Nov 2024 (merger of RADEEO and the ONEE local branch); Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceima 2025 (start date disputed); Fès-Meknès 15 Jul 2025; Rabat-Salé-Kénitra 1 Jun 2025 (another report says 1 Apr 2025); Béni Mellal-Khénifra and Drâa-Tafilalet planned 1 Aug 2025 (go-live not confirmed); Laâyoune-Sakia El Hamra and Dakhla-Oued Ed-Dahab 6 Oct 2025; Guelmim-Oued Noun 6 Oct 2025.
- A press search summary (Hespress/Le Matin, not fetched) says seven SRMs were in service by end Sep 2025: Casablanca-Settat, Souss-Massa, Marrakech-Safi, Oriental, Rabat-Salé-Kénitra, Fès-Meknès and Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceima. This supports the Oriental and Tanger rows as operational by that date.
- Le Matin reported all 12 SRMs operational by end 2025 and the ONEE activity transfer finalised in Nov 2025 (secondary, not checked against an official text).
- **Logos: one official-hosted logo verified (SRM Souss-Massa).** The other 11 are NOT FOUND.
- **Official websites: none verified.** See section 3.

## 5. Rural and other exceptions

- Redal retains part of the Rabat-Salé-Kénitra perimeter until 2028 (Medias24, Jun 2025).
- Amendis is reported as still holding some perimeters in its former areas; the handover status is not confirmed.
- Al Omrane Drâa-Tafilalet is a separate urban-development entity, not the water operator.
- The Oriental SRM absorbed the ONEE local branch; ONEE's roles elsewhere, including rural water and electricity, were not checked.
- Rural electrification and rural water supply were not researched. Open gap.
